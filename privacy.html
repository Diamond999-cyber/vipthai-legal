VIP Thai Cuisine - Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

VIP Thai Cuisine is operated by PSMAMAS LLC ("VIP Thai Cuisine," "we," "us," or "our"), located at 3520 Seven Bridges Drive, Suite 103, Woodridge, IL 60517. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to our restaurant operations and to our text message (SMS) program for employees.

1. Information We Collect

  • Employee text messaging: the mobile phone number and name that an employee provides when signing our written SMS consent form.
  • Messaging records: the content, date, and delivery status of the text messages we send, and any replies such as STOP or HELP.
  • General business information that you choose to give us when you contact us, such as your name, email address, and message.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information above only to:

  • Send internal operational text messages (for example, daily market-list reminders listing ingredients to buy for the restaurant).
  • Respond to replies and requests, including HELP and STOP.
  • Keep records of consent and comply with legal and carrier requirements.
  • Operate and improve our restaurant.

We do not send marketing or promotional text messages from our text messaging program.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number or text messaging opt-in information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

We share information only with service providers that help us deliver our text messages (for example, our messaging platform provider and wireless carriers), and only as needed to deliver those messages. We may also disclose information when required by law.

4. Text Message (SMS) Program

  • Who receives messages: employees of VIP Thai Cuisine who have signed our written SMS consent form.
  • Message frequency: about 1 message per day. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Opt out: reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. After you reply STOP, you will receive no further messages unless you opt in again.
  • Help: reply HELP, or contact us using the details below.
  • Consent is not a condition of employment.

5. Data Retention and Security

We keep consent records and messaging records only as long as needed for the purposes above and to meet legal obligations. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Choices

You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us below.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.
